5 Days / 4 Nights Itinerary

Hunza Valley Explorer

Day 1

Departure from Islamabad/Gilgit, scenic Karakoram Highway drive, Rakaposhi viewpoint lunch, arrival at Karimabad.

Day 2

Baltit Fort and Altit Fort guided historical walk, traditional Balti lunch, sunset at Eagle's Nest overlooking Hunza and Nagar valleys.

Day 3

Attabad Lake motorboat cruise, Passu Glacier viewpoint, Hussaini suspension bridge crossing, and Passu Cones photography.

Day 4

Excursion to Khunjerab Pass (Pakistan-China Border, 4,693m) - world highest paved border. Return to Hunza.

Day 5

Local organic bazaar shopping for dry fruits & gemstones; return transfer to Gilgit airport or Islamabad.

Skardu & Deosai Plains

Day 1-2

Arrival in Skardu, Indus-Shigar junction, visit historic Shigar Fort and upper Kachura Lake.

Day 3

Shangrila Lower Kachura Lake and afternoon dune walk across Sarfaranga & Katpana Cold Desert.

Full day 4x4 expedition into Deosai National Park: Ali Malik pass, Sheosar Lake, brown bear sanctuary.

Sadpara Lake scenic tour, Manthal Buddha rock carving, and authentic Balti trout dinner.

Day 7

Departure flight from Skardu International Airport.

6 Days / 5 Nights Itinerary

Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat

Day 1

Drive from Islamabad/Gilgit via Chilas to Raikot Bridge on the Indus River.

Day 2

Thrilling 4x4 mountain jeep track to Tato village, followed by 3-hour escorted alpine hike into Fairy Meadows.

Day 3

Acclimatization day at Fairy Meadows with wooden cabin bonfire and direct views of Nanga Parbat.

Day 4

Scenic guided day-trek to Beyal Camp and Nanga Parbat Base Camp Viewpoint. Return to Fairy Meadows.

Day 5-6

Hike down to Tato, jeep descent to Raikot, return highway transfer to Islamabad/Gilgit.
